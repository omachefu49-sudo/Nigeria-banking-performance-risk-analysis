# Nigerian Banking Sector Performance & Risk Analysis

**Project Status:** 🟡 Planned

An independent financial data analytics project examining the performance, operational efficiency and financial risk profiles of selected Nigerian commercial banks.

## 1. Problem Statement

Revenue and profit growth do not necessarily indicate strong operational efficiency or financial stability.

Investors, regulators and other stakeholders need to understand how effectively banks generate income, manage operating costs, maintain asset quality and withstand financial risks.

This project aims to provide a comparative assessment of Nigerian banks using publicly available financial and macroeconomic data.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Evaluate the profitability of selected Nigerian banks.
- Assess operational efficiency and cost management.
- Examine asset quality, liquidity and capital adequacy.
- Investigate trends in non-performing loans and credit risk.
- Compare financial performance across selected banks.
- Explore the relationship between macroeconomic conditions and banking sector performance.

## 3. Data Sources

- [Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/)
- [CBN Statistical Database](https://statistics.cbn.gov.ng/)
- [National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/)
- [Access Bank Financial Reports](https://www.accessbankplc.com/investor-relations/financial-reports)
- [UBA Investor Relations](https://www.ubagroup.com/investors/investor-relations/)
- Annual reports and financial statements of selected Nigerian banks.

## 4. Analytical Approach

- Financial statement analysis.
- Profitability and efficiency ratio analysis.
- Asset quality and credit risk assessment.
- Liquidity and capital adequacy analysis.
- Comparative and sector benchmarking.
- Multi-year trend analysis.
- DuPont analysis and financial risk assessment.
